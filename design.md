# Design system — AI Ascent Studio

This file defines how everything I make should look. It applies to slides,
proposals, documents and web pages.

Written by hand from the existing aiascent.ai identity. Not generated, not
copied from another brand.

---

## PRINCIPLE

Calm, clean, easy on the eye, easy to learn from.

Someone should be able to read a slide from across a room and a document at
arm's length without effort. Nothing should feel loud, busy or urgent.
Restraint is the house style.

---

## COLOUR

Violet is the brand. It appears as accent only — never as a background fill,
never as a gradient, never as a wash behind text.

Primary violets (from the Fibonacci dot mark):
- Deep violet   #26215C   headings, the mark, strong emphasis
- Mid violet    #534AB7   links, active states, primary buttons
- Light violet  #7F77DD   secondary accents, hover states, small marks

Pastel:
- Pale lilac    #EFEDFB   soft button fills, quiet blocks behind text,
                          anywhere a surface should be gentle rather than loud

Surface and text:
- Background    #FDFDFC   off-white, very slightly warm. Never pure #FFFFFF —
                          pure white is harsh over long reading and the whole
                          point here is that reading is easy
- Body text     #1A1826   near-black with a violet cast, never pure #000
- Muted text    #6B6880   captions, labels, secondary lines
- Hairline      #E8E6F0   borders, rules, table lines

(Body, muted and hairline are PROPOSED — derived from the deep violet so they
sit with the brand rather than fighting it. Replace with the real values from
aiascent.ai if they differ.)

Rules:
- Off-white is the default surface. Pale lilac is the only alternative.
  Do not invent others.
- Vibrant violet goes in small deliberate places — the mark, a link, a single
  emphasis. Large surfaces take the pastel. Saturation is earned, not spread.
- One accent violet per screen or slide. Not all three at once.
- Colour carries meaning, not decoration. If it is not marking hierarchy or
  state, it should not be coloured.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

- Heading face:   Source Serif 4
- Body face:      IBM Plex Sans

(Both PROPOSED, not confirmed from the site. Both are open licence and free to
self-host. A quiet serif for headings over a humanist sans for body reads
editorial and calm — it is also the fastest way to not look like every other
AI product, which all use the same geometric sans. Replace with the real faces
from aiascent.ai if they differ.)

Rules that matter more than the faces:
- Line height generous — 1.5 or more for body text.
- Line length capped around 65-75 characters. Long lines are the single most
  common reason a document feels tiring.
- Three levels of hierarchy maximum on any one view. More than three reads as
  noise rather than structure.
- Size does the work of hierarchy, not weight and colour piled on top of it.
- Left-aligned. No centred body text, no justified text.

---

## SPACING AND LAYOUT

- Generous whitespace is the point, not wasted space. When in doubt, more.
- Consistent rhythm — spacing should come from a repeating scale, not
  eyeballed per element.
- One idea per slide. If a slide has two arguments, it is two slides.
- Content sits in a clear column. Nothing bleeds edge to edge.

---

## COMPONENTS

- Buttons: pale lilac fill with deep violet text for the gentle default, or
  solid mid violet with off-white text where a page needs one strong action.
  Modest corner radius. No shadow.
- Cards: defined by whitespace and a hairline border, not by shadow or fill.
- Links: mid violet, underlined on hover.
- Tables: horizontal rules only. No zebra striping, no vertical grid lines.

---

## NEVER

This list does more work than everything above it.

- No gradients. None. Especially not violet-to-blue — that is the generic AI
  brand look and it is the exact thing this identity avoids.
- No neon or saturated brights.
- No dark backgrounds.
- No drop shadows or glows.
- No stock icons, no icon sets used as decoration.
- No emoji.
- No stock photography of people at laptops.
- No decorative background shapes, blobs, meshes or particles.
- No animation that moves without being asked to.
- No more than one accent colour per view.
- No centred paragraphs of body text.
- No corporate filler language — "bespoke solutions", "cutting-edge",
  "empowering". Plain words only.

---

## VOICE IN LAYOUTS

Headings are plain statements, not slogans. Sentence case, not Title Case.
Short sentences. If a line sounds like a company wrote it, cut it.

The site headline is the tone reference: "From name to working product."

---

## DO NOT INVENT

If a value is missing here, ask rather than filling the gap. Anything marked
PROPOSED above is unconfirmed and should be checked against the live site.
